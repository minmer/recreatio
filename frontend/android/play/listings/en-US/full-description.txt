REcreatio is the workplace of parishes and communities — groups, choirs, youth groups, pastoral teams — and their members. The app is in Polish.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Calendar: your events and your groups' events by day, week, month or as a list; recurring events; pin the people who need to be there.
• Bookings: appointments with the priest, rooms, a retreat house — confirmed by the host or the parish office.
• Tasks: what needs doing, with a time window — you see at a glance what is due now.
• Chat: group conversations with replies, reactions, voice messages and files; message editing with a change history.
• Groups and roles: who belongs where and who leads what, with permissions granted by the community.
• Forms and registrations run by the parish or community.

ENCRYPTED ON YOUR DEVICE
Your password never leaves the phone. It derives a key with which the phone encrypts names, personal data, message content, attachments, private events and notes before anything is sent. The service stores this data but cannot read it.
The device key is protected by the Android Keystore. The app makes no cloud backups.

THE LIMITS OF ENCRYPTION — HONESTLY
• The service sees metadata: account name, group names, who belongs to which group, event times, public titles (e.g. Mass times), when and how much was written.
• A lost password cannot be recovered. Without the password and without a kept key on a device, nobody — including us — can restore access to encrypted data.
• Whoever has your unlocked phone with an open session sees what you see.

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
No ads, no analytics, no tracking libraries. The app talks only to the REcreatio service. It also works without Google services (e.g. on GrapheneOS).

ACCOUNT
An account is required; you create it in the app (16+). You can delete it yourself at any time: Konto → Usunięcie konta.
